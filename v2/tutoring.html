Use the Explanation to Choose the Next Teaching Move

My math and English tutoring at StarHill Pathways involved supporting Charlottesville school students. This session assumes a learner who knows basic arithmetic and can read a short paragraph, with uncertain confidence in fraction units and supporting an inference.

The goal is to explain a fraction calculation and connect a reading conclusion to a specific detail. A correct guess does not establish understanding; the learner's explanation determines whether to model, prompt or move to independent practice.

Allocate 5 minutes to welcome and goals, 8 to a diagnostic, 15 to guided math, 15 to guided reading, 12 to independent practice and 5 to the exit check. These blocks total 60 minutes.

Make Common Units Visible Before Adding Fractions

Diagnostic Write one-half in eighths and explain why the amount stays the same.

Worked Answer 1/2 = 4/8. Each half is divided into four equal parts, producing eight parts in the whole.

Guided Task A class plants 3/4 of a garden on Monday and 1/8 on Tuesday. What fraction is planted, and what remains?

3/4 = 6/8. Then 6/8 + 1/8 = 7/8 planted, leaving 1/8.

If the learner adds denominators, draw one whole divided into eight equal parts. Shade six and then one. Ask what one shaded part represents before retrying.

Independent Check Calculate 1/3 + 1/6 and explain the units. The key is 2/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.

Connect the Conclusion to Evidence in the Passage

Prompt What does Lena propose, and which detail supports her choice?

Worked Answer She proposes repairing the can and testing a shorter route. The cracked can and students stopping when their shoes became wet support addressing the equipment and process.

Independent Check Has the passage established that the plan works? No. The group has not yet checked the Friday result.

Advance Only When the Response Demonstrates Understanding

Without a model, calculate 3/8 + 1/4 and explain the conversion. Then state Lena's proposal and connect one passage detail to it. The math answer is 5/8 because 1/4 = 2/8.

Four-point exit rubric, one point per criterion
CriterionObservable Evidence
Common UnitsConverts 1/4 to 2/8 and explains equal-sized parts.
Correct TotalFinds 5/8 without adding the denominators.
Proposal UnderstoodIdentifies repairing the can and trying a shorter route.
Evidence ConnectedNames a relevant detail and explains how it supports the proposal.

Advance at 3/4 or better only when the correct-total and evidence-connected criteria both pass. Otherwise, begin the next session with the missing skill and a fresh question. Record the response and level of prompting before drawing conclusions about progress.
