Recruitment and Community Access

The expo was intended to give underrepresented entrepreneurs at UVA greater exposure and access to potential customers. My outreach helped businesses understand the opportunity and decide whether to participate. That recruiting work is the starting point for this case.

The retrospective playbook gives each channel a purpose and a clear follow-up. It makes the work behind a sign-up visible, including the questions an owner needs answered before committing.

From First Contact to Event Readiness

The proposed tracker uses one record per business, preserves contact history and gives the next action an owner. Interest starts a conversation; confirmation requires an explicit decision and an available place.

Synthetic Capacity Check: Twelve confirmed businesses use twelve of fourteen places. Three open invitations remain for two places. Confirming all three would create one excess commitment; the event lead must verify more space or communicate a waitlist.

What I Would Measure Next: Response and confirmation rates with explicit denominators, time to the next follow-up, and unresolved participation questions. This portfolio does not include original campaign logs; historical conversion rates and channel performance remain unverified.
