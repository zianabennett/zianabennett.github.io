Ask What the Available Evidence Can Establish

My original USOAR research-assistant role with Professor Kisha Lashley focused on Jamaica’s track-and-field ecosystem. This portfolio extension returns to that subject through a narrower question about documented institutional support.

The corpus contains eight selected institutions, eleven primary-source pages and nineteen institution-function assignments. It covers school competition, tertiary institutions, national administration and cross-sport support. The institutions were chosen for functional variety, not sampled randomly.

The Research Decision Map explicitly documented functions. Keep programme existence, actual use and athlete outcomes as separate questions.

Every Coded Observation Needs a Traceable Source

The codebook defines eight functions, including competition, training, coach education, facilities, finance, governance, welfare and clean sport. Each assignment needs an observation and a registered source. An institution can receive several codes, but its name or reputation is not enough to justify one.

A missing code means that a function was not established by the selected pages. It does not prove the function is absent. This distinction prevents incomplete documentation from becoming an unsupported institutional judgment.

Look Beyond Competition Without Claiming a Complete System

Four of the eight institutions have documented competition access or delivery. Facilities and welfare each appear for three; training and coach education each appear for two. These overlapping counts describe the sample and are not national coverage estimates.

The tertiary institutions also have different documented roles. The selected UTech and UWI Mona pages cover direct athlete programmes and supporting services; the G C Foster page concerns coach development. That distinction gives a follow-up study two different lines of inquiry rather than one generic university stage.

Cross-sport bodies add welfare, funding and clean-sport functions. Their inclusion broadens the questions worth investigating, while their remit prevents a defensible conversion into track-and-field spending or participation totals.

Study a Transition Before Drawing an Arrow

The next useful study would choose a defined transition, such as leaving school athletics, and document which services athletes actually received. Consented interviews or appropriate administrative records could examine eligibility, access, referral steps and continuity.

Why That Step Matters Drawing a pathway between organizations would imply relationships the current sources do not establish. The present map helps frame a study; it cannot demonstrate a coordinated development pipeline.

Website detail and freshness vary. One UWI page retains an older availability notice, and the corpus excludes private clubs, families and overseas collegiate systems. There was no independent second coder. Reproducible output checks catch structural data errors, but they do not independently validate institutional claims.
