A little about me

Useful work starts
with understanding people.

My interest in accessible systems started before I was working on product problems. Supporting immigration-related work made me pay attention to the confusion a process can create for the person on the other side of it. Student leadership and tutoring reinforced the value of a clear explanation and a specific next step.

I studied Commerce at the University of Virginia’s McIntire School, concentrating in Management and IT with a Business Analytics track. I bring that business perspective to questions about how a service should work and how a team can move it forward.

I am interested in product and business operations roles where careful analysis stays connected to the people using a service. I also enjoy exploring small-business ideas and learning Spanish.
