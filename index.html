A little about me

Business context.
Careful execution.

I studied Commerce at the University of Virginia's McIntire School, with a Management and IT concentration and a Business Analytics track. My experience has taken me from AI research and product planning to student leadership and fundraising.

I am drawn to work that improves how people and small businesses navigate complicated systems. I like asking what a request really means, finding the information that matters, and making the result easier for someone else to use.

Outside work, I enjoy youth tutoring, learning Spanish, and exploring small-business ideas. I also studied abroad at ESADE in Barcelona.
