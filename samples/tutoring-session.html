Math and reading session design

A complete tutoring session with diagnostic choices, worked answers and an evidence-based exit check.

A 60-minute lesson with a decision at each step

This new session design draws on my math and English tutoring at StarHill Pathways. It assumes a learner who knows basic arithmetic and can read a short paragraph, with uncertain confidence in fraction units and supporting an inference. The target is to explain a fraction calculation and connect a reading conclusion to a specific detail.

Timing Allocate 5 minutes to welcome and goals, 8 to a diagnostic, 15 to guided math, 15 to guided reading, 12 to independent practice and 5 to the exit check. These blocks total 60 minutes.

Math task and worked answer

Diagnostic Write one-half in eighths and explain why the amount stays the same.

Answer: 1/2 = 4/8. Each half is divided into four equal parts, producing eight parts in the whole.

Guided task A class plants 3/4 of a garden on Monday and 1/8 on Tuesday. What fraction is planted, and what remains?

3/4 = 6/8. Then 6/8 + 1/8 = 7/8 planted, leaving 1/8.

If the learner adds denominators, draw one whole divided into eight equal parts. Shade six and then one. Ask what one shaded part represents before retrying.

Independent check Calculate 1/3 + 1/6 and explain the units. Key 2/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.

Reading passage and discussion

After school, Lena checked the class garden. The soil beneath the bean plants was dry, although the tomatoes near the tap were damp. Everyone on the volunteer list had arrived that week. The watering can had a crack, and several students said they stopped when their shoes became wet. Lena asked the group to repair the can and test a shorter watering route before adding more volunteers. On Friday, they would check the soil again and decide whether the change had helped.

Prompt What does Lena propose, and which detail supports her choice?

She proposes repairing the can and testing a shorter route. The cracked can and students stopping when their shoes became wet support addressing the equipment and process.

Independent check Has the passage established that the plan works? Key No. The group has not yet checked the Friday result.

Exit check and next-session rule

Without a model, calculate 3/8 + 1/4 and explain the conversion. Then state Lena's proposal and connect one passage detail to it. The math answer is 5/8 because 1/4 = 2/8.

One point for eachObservable evidence
Common unitsConverts 1/4 to 2/8 and explains equal-sized parts.
Correct totalFinds 5/8 without adding the denominators.
Proposal understoodIdentifies repairing the can and trying a shorter route.
Evidence connectedNames a relevant detail and explains how it supports the proposal.

Advance at 3/4 or better only when the correct-total and evidence-connected criteria both pass. Otherwise, begin the next session with the missing skill and a fresh question. Record the response and level of prompting; this plan claims no historical student score improvement.
